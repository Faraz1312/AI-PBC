Welcome to Part 2: Real Numbers — Chapter 1 of your NCERT Math textbook.

Let’s begin gently.

The word ‘real’ here doesn’t mean fake versus real — it means all the numbers you’ve learned till now, combined.

Real numbers include both rational and irrational numbers.

Now let’s zoom into prime numbers.

A prime number has exactly two factors — one, and itself. That means it can’t be divided evenly by anything else.

Examples are two, three, five, seven, and so on.

Now here comes an important concept — the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic.

It means: Every number that’s not a prime can be broken down into a product of prime numbers.

For example, sixty is two times two times three times five.

Now, let’s learn about HCF — Highest Common Factor.

Suppose you have two numbers. HCF is the largest number that divides both.

Like eight and twelve. Their HCF is four.

Now LCM — Least Common Multiple.

It’s the smallest number that both numbers can divide into.

For three and four, the LCM is twelve.

Let that sink in...
